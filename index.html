About The P2V

The P2V (Places to Visit) helps you discover, track, and plan visits to amazing places around you — from popular landmarks to hidden local gems.

  • Interactive map powered by Leaflet
  • Smart filters by distance & popularity
  • Save places with visit status
  • One-tap directions via Google Maps

Built with passion by Aadil Salmani, blending geospatial intelligence, clean design, and real-world travel needs.
