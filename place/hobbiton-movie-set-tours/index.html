Hobbiton™ Movie Set Tours

📍 New Zealand

Overview

Located at coordinates -37.87211482, 175.6828667, this site is renowned for its vibrant depiction of the Shire from J.R.R. Tolkien's literary works, specifically "The Hobbit" and "The Lord of the Rings." Nestled in the picturesque rolling hills of New Zealand’s Waikato region, the Hobbiton Movie Set offers a unique glimpse into the world of hobbits, featuring meticulously crafted hobbit holes, lush gardens, and iconic landmarks like the Green Dragon Inn and the Party Tree. The set is not only a film location but also a testament to the artistry involved in bringing Tolkien's universe to life.

Why Visit

  • Explore over 44 unique hobbit holes, each with distinct characteristics and vibrant gardens.
  • Visit the Green Dragon Inn, where visitors can enjoy a complimentary beverage in a setting reminiscent of the films.
  • Discover the intricate details of the set, including the Party Tree and the Stone Circle, which add to the authenticity of the experience.
  • Learn about the filmmaking process through guided tours that provide insights into the set design and construction.
  • Experience the breathtaking landscapes that surround the site, ideal for photography and leisurely walks.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to the set can embark on guided tours that last approximately two hours. Each tour includes access to the entire movie set, with knowledgeable guides sharing stories about the filming process and the creative vision behind the iconic locations. Guests can stroll through the charming pathways, view the intricately designed hobbit holes, and appreciate the surrounding farmland that enhances the enchanting ambiance of the Shire. The experience often concludes at the Green Dragon Inn, where guests can relax and savor a drink while soaking in the atmosphere of this beloved fictional world.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit, it is advisable to wear comfortable footwear, as the terrain can be uneven in some areas. Bringing a camera is highly recommended to capture the stunning views and intricate details throughout the set. Visitors should consider checking the weather forecast, as conditions can vary; dressing in layers is suggested for comfort. Additionally, booking tours in advance is encouraged to secure a spot, especially during peak tourist seasons.

Nearby Places

In close proximity to the set, visitors can explore the charming town of Matamata, which serves as a gateway to Hobbiton. The town offers a variety of local shops and eateries, providing a taste of New Zealand's rural culture. Additionally, the scenic landscapes of the Waikato region are home to various outdoor activities, including hiking and cycling trails that showcase the area’s natural beauty. The Waitomo Caves, known for their stunning limestone formations and glowworm displays, are also a worthwhile excursion not far from the movie set.
