AURA SKYPOOL Dubai

📍 UAE

Overview

AURA SKYPOOL, situated at coordinates 25.11402496, 55.14026684, is an innovative swimming pool located in the United Arab Emirates. This unique attraction boasts the title of the world's highest 360-degree infinity pool. Perched atop a skyscraper, it offers stunning panoramic views of the cityscape and beyond. The pool is designed to provide an immersive experience, allowing visitors to relax while enjoying breathtaking vistas of iconic landmarks and the Arabian Gulf.

Why Visit

  • Experience the world's highest infinity pool, offering an uninterrupted view of Dubai's skyline.
  • Enjoy a unique architectural design that combines luxury and innovation.
  • Relax in a serene environment, ideal for swimming and soaking up the sun.
  • Take advantage of the poolside amenities, including lounges and refreshments.
  • Capture stunning photographs against the backdrop of the vibrant Dubai landscape.

Visitor Experience

The visitor experience at AURA SKYPOOL is defined by its remarkable setting and high-quality amenities. As guests arrive, they are greeted with expansive views that stretch across Dubai, including famous landmarks such as the Burj Khalifa and the Palm Jumeirah. The pool itself is designed for comfort and relaxation, featuring comfortable loungers and shaded areas for those seeking respite from the sun. The atmosphere is typically tranquil, with a focus on providing a luxurious yet welcoming environment for both relaxation and social interaction.

Travel Tips

To make the most of your visit to AURA SKYPOOL, consider arriving early in the day to secure a prime spot with unobstructed views. It’s advisable to check the weather forecast ahead of your visit, as clear skies will enhance the experience. Bring along essentials such as sunscreen, a hat, and swimwear to ensure a comfortable day by the pool. Additionally, keep your camera ready to capture the stunning views from various angles, especially during sunset when the city lights begin to twinkle.

Nearby Places

While visiting AURA SKYPOOL, you may want to explore other attractions in the vicinity. The iconic Dubai Marina is nearby, offering a vibrant atmosphere with a variety of dining and shopping options. Additionally, consider visiting the nearby Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) for a lively beachfront experience. The area is also home to various art galleries and cultural exhibitions, providing a rich insight into the local culture and contemporary art scene.
