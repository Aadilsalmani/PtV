Sri Dalada Maligawa

Sri Dalada Maligawa

📍 Sri Lanka

Overview

Sri Dalada Maligawa, also known as the Temple of the Tooth, is a significant Buddhist temple located in Kandy, Sri Lanka. Situated at coordinates 7.293606667, 80.64132331, this revered site is renowned for housing a relic believed to be a tooth of the Buddha. The temple is an essential pilgrimage site for Buddhists and is deeply intertwined with the cultural and historical identity of Sri Lanka. Built in the 16th century, it showcases exquisite architecture and is surrounded by an aura of spirituality and reverence.

Why Visit

  • The temple houses the sacred relic of the Buddha's tooth, which is a central focus of worship and pilgrimage.
  • Architectural beauty: The site features intricate carvings, beautiful frescoes, and a stunning golden canopy.
  • The annual Esala Perahera festival, celebrated in July or August, is a vibrant cultural event featuring traditional dance and processions.
  • The serene setting by Kandy Lake enhances the spiritual atmosphere of the complex.
  • Visitors can explore the museum within the temple complex, which displays ancient artifacts and religious items.

Visitor Experience

Entering the temple, visitors are greeted by a tranquil ambiance, complemented by the sound of chanting monks and the flickering of oil lamps. The temple grounds are meticulously maintained, offering a peaceful retreat for both worshippers and travelers. Visitors may witness daily rituals, including the offering of flowers and incense, which provide a glimpse into the spiritual practices of Buddhism. The golden casket that houses the tooth relic is prominently displayed within the temple, attracting the attention and devotion of countless admirers. Additionally, the surrounding gardens and the view of Kandy Lake create a picturesque environment for reflection and contemplation.

Travel Tips

When visiting, it is advisable to dress modestly as a sign of respect for the sacred nature of the temple. Shoulders and knees should be covered, and shoes must be removed before entering certain areas. Arriving early in the day can help avoid crowds and allow for a more personal experience of the temple's serene atmosphere. It is also useful to familiarize oneself with local customs and practices to enhance the visit. For those interested in photography, the best light for capturing the temple's beauty is during the golden hour, shortly after sunrise or before sunset.

Nearby Places

Adjacent to the temple, visitors can explore Kandy Lake, which offers scenic walking paths and a peaceful setting. The Royal Palace of Kandy, located nearby, provides insight into the history of the Kandyan kingdom. Additionally, the Kandy Museum, found within the temple complex, houses a collection of historical artifacts that complement the cultural experience. A short distance from the temple is the Bahirawakanda Vihara Buddha Statue, which offers panoramic views of Kandy and is another notable landmark for visitors to explore.
