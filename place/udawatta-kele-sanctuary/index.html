Udawatta Kele Sanctuary

Udawatta Kele Sanctuary

📍 Sri Lanka

Overview

Udawatta Kele Sanctuary is a significant nature reserve located in Kandy, Sri Lanka. Covering an area of approximately 104 hectares, this sanctuary serves as a vital green lung for the city and is rich in biodiversity. The sanctuary is situated on the hills adjacent to the Temple of the Tooth, one of the most revered Buddhist sites in the country. Established in the 19th century as a royal forest, it played a crucial role in the ecosystem of the region, offering habitat to numerous flora and fauna.

Why Visit

  • Biodiversity: The sanctuary is home to a variety of plant species, including many endemic trees, and serves as a habitat for numerous bird species, making it a prime destination for bird watchers.
  • Historical Significance: Once a royal hunting ground, the sanctuary is steeped in history, with remnants of old pathways and structures that reflect its past as a retreat for the Kandyan royalty.
  • Scenic Trails: There are several well-maintained trails that wind through the dense forest, allowing visitors to explore the rich natural surroundings while enjoying panoramic views of Kandy.
  • Peaceful Environment: The sanctuary offers a tranquil escape from the bustling city, making it an ideal spot for relaxation and contemplation amidst nature.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to Udawatta Kele Sanctuary can engage in a range of activities, from leisurely walks to more vigorous hikes. The sanctuary's trails are marked and allow for exploration of its diverse ecosystems, where one might encounter various species of birds, including the elusive purple-faced langur and numerous butterflies. The quiet atmosphere is punctuated by the sounds of nature, providing an immersive experience in the heart of Kandy. Additionally, guided tours are available for those seeking a deeper understanding of the sanctuary’s ecology and history, enhancing the overall experience.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to Udawatta Kele Sanctuary, consider wearing comfortable shoes suitable for hiking, as some trails can be steep and uneven. Early morning visits are recommended for optimal birdwatching opportunities, as many species are most active during this time. Bring water and snacks, as facilities within the sanctuary are limited. Additionally, it is advisable to check the weather forecast, as the area can experience sudden rain showers. Respect the natural environment by following designated paths and avoiding littering.

Nearby Places

Close to Udawatta Kele Sanctuary, the Temple of the Tooth is a must-visit site, renowned for its sacred relic of the Buddha. Nearby, the Royal Palace of Kandy offers insights into the region’s royal history and architecture. The picturesque Kandy Lake, situated adjacent to the temple, provides a serene backdrop for leisurely strolls. Additionally, the Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, just a short drive away, feature an extensive collection of tropical plants and trees, making it another worthwhile stop for nature enthusiasts.
