Khanvel Resort

📍 Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu(India)

Overview

Khanvel Resort is located in the serene and lush surroundings of the Western Ghats in India, specifically in the Dadra and Nagar Haveli region. This destination is characterized by thick forests, diverse flora and fauna, and a tranquil atmosphere, making it a popular spot for nature lovers and those seeking respite from urban life. With coordinates at 20.12908565 latitude and 73.05933777 longitude, the resort offers a peaceful retreat amidst the natural beauty of the region.

Why Visit

  • Rich Biodiversity: The area surrounding Khanvel is home to diverse wildlife and numerous plant species, making it an ideal location for eco-tourism.
  • Adventure Activities: Visitors can engage in various outdoor activities such as trekking, cycling, and nature walks through the verdant landscapes.
  • Cultural Experiences: The region is adorned with tribal culture, allowing visitors to explore local traditions, crafts, and cuisine.
  • Peaceful Environment: The resort's setting provides a calm escape from the hustle and bustle of city life, encouraging relaxation and rejuvenation.
  • Proximity to Waterfalls: Nearby waterfalls, such as the picturesque Vagh Falls, attract nature enthusiasts and photographers alike.

Visitor Experience

At Khanvel Resort, guests can enjoy a unique blend of recreational activities and serene environments. The lush greenery and cool climate enhance the overall experience, allowing visitors to immerse themselves in nature. Guided nature walks provide insights into the local ecosystem, while adventure seekers can partake in exhilarating treks and cycling routes that showcase the stunning landscapes. Cultural tours offer glimpses into the traditional lifestyles of the indigenous communities in the area, enriching the visitor's understanding of the region's heritage.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to Khanvel Resort, consider the best times to travel, which are typically during the cooler months from October to March. This period offers pleasant weather for outdoor activities. It is advisable to wear comfortable clothing and sturdy footwear, especially for trekking and exploring the natural surroundings. As the region is rich in wildlife, carrying binoculars can enhance the experience for birdwatchers. Additionally, making arrangements for guided tours can provide valuable insights into the local flora and fauna, as well as cultural experiences.

Nearby Places

In addition to the attractions at Khanvel Resort, visitors can explore several nearby sites. The Dudhni Lake is a serene spot for picnics and water sports, offering stunning views of the surrounding hills. The tribal villages within the region provide opportunities to learn about local customs and crafts. For those interested in history, the ancient forts and temples scattered throughout the area reflect the rich cultural heritage of Dadra and Nagar Haveli. Exploring these nearby attractions enhances the overall experience, allowing visitors to appreciate the diverse offerings of this captivating region.
