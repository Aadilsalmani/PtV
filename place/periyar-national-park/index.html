Periyar National Park, Kerala(India)

Kerala(India)

Overview

Periyar National Park is a renowned protected area located in the Western Ghats of Kerala, India. Encompassing approximately 925 square kilometers, this national park is famous for its rich biodiversity and stunning landscapes, characterized by dense evergreen and deciduous forests, rolling hills, and the picturesque Periyar Lake. Established in 1950 as a wildlife sanctuary and later designated as a national park in 1982, it serves as a vital habitat for numerous threatened and endemic species, making it an essential sanctuary for wildlife conservation.

Why Visit

  • Diverse Wildlife: The park is home to a variety of species, including elephants, tigers, bison, and a multitude of birds. It is particularly notable for its population of Asian elephants, which can often be seen near the lake.
  • Scenic Beauty: The landscape offers breathtaking views, from the tranquil waters of Periyar Lake to the misty hills surrounding it, making it a perfect spot for photography and nature enthusiasts.
  • Cultural Significance: The park is situated near various tribal settlements, allowing visitors to gain insights into the local culture and traditions of the indigenous communities.
  • Ecotourism Opportunities: Various eco-friendly activities such as guided nature walks, boat rides on the lake, and bamboo rafting provide immersive experiences in the park's natural environment.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to this national park can engage in a variety of activities that enhance their experience of the natural world. Guided nature treks allow for exploration of the park’s diverse flora and fauna, while boat rides on Periyar Lake offer opportunities to observe wildlife from a unique vantage point. The park's rich biodiversity is complemented by well-maintained trails that cater to both casual walkers and avid trekkers. Birdwatching is particularly rewarding, as the park is home to over 260 species of birds, making it a hotspot for ornithologists and bird lovers alike. Additionally, the presence of local tribes adds a cultural dimension to the visit, with opportunities to learn about their traditional lifestyles and practices.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to Periyar National Park, consider the best times to go; the cooler months between October and March are ideal for wildlife viewing. It is advisable to wear comfortable clothing and sturdy shoes suitable for walking on uneven terrain. Carrying binoculars and a camera can enhance the experience of observing wildlife and capturing the park's stunning vistas. Visitors should also remain respectful of wildlife rules and guidelines to ensure both their safety and the conservation of the park’s ecosystem. Lastly, staying hydrated and carrying some snacks can be beneficial during long excursions in the park.

Nearby Places

Nearby attractions include the scenic town of Thekkady, which serves as a gateway to the national park. The town offers additional activities such as spice plantation tours and local markets showcasing Kerala’s rich agricultural produce. Additionally, visitors can explore the nearby Kumily, known for its vibrant cultural scene and opportunities to engage with local artisans. Nature enthusiasts may also wish to visit the surrounding hills and valleys of the Western Ghats, recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, where further trekking and wildlife observation await.
