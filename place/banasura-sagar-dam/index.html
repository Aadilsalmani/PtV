Banasura Sagar Dam

Banasura Sagar Dam

📍 Kerala(India)

Overview

Banasura Sagar Dam, located in the Wayanad district of Kerala, India, is the largest earthen dam in India and a prominent tourist attraction in the region. Nestled amidst the Banasura Hills, the dam is constructed on the tributary of the Kabini River and spans an impressive length of approximately 3.8 kilometers. The dam was completed in 1979 and primarily serves the purpose of irrigation and hydroelectric power generation. It is named after Banasura, a character from Hindu mythology known for his enormous stature and strength.

Why Visit

  • Experience the scenic vistas of the Western Ghats, with lush greenery and serene waters surrounding the dam.
  • Engage in trekking and nature walks in the nearby Banasura Hill Resort, offering trails that cater to different skill levels.
  • Enjoy boating activities on the reservoir, with options for paddle boats and speed boats, providing an immersive experience on the water.
  • Explore the diverse flora and fauna in the area, as the dam is situated in a region rich in biodiversity and home to various species of birds and animals.
  • Visit the nearby Banasura Hill, which is the second highest peak in Wayanad, offering panoramic views and opportunities for adventure enthusiasts.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to Banasura Sagar Dam often find themselves captivated by the serene ambiance and stunning landscapes. The dam's vast water body reflects the sky and surrounding hills, creating a picturesque backdrop ideal for photography. The cool breeze and tranquil environment make it a perfect spot for relaxation. Boating facilities are available, allowing guests to explore the reservoir while soaking in the natural beauty. Additionally, the surrounding area is dotted with tea plantations and small villages, providing insight into local culture and lifestyle. Visitors can also enjoy local cuisine at nearby eateries, enriching their experience of this scenic destination.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to Banasura Sagar Dam, it is advisable to check the weather conditions, as the area experiences heavy rainfall during the monsoon season, which may affect accessibility. Wear comfortable footwear, especially if you intend to explore trekking trails or walk around the dam area. Carrying water and snacks is recommended, as facilities may be limited. Additionally, visiting early in the morning or late in the afternoon can provide the best lighting for photography and a more pleasant experience away from the midday heat. Respecting the natural environment and local customs is encouraged to ensure a positive impact on the community and surroundings.

Nearby Places

Several attractions are in close proximity to Banasura Sagar Dam. The Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary offers a chance to spot various wildlife species in their natural habitat. Another nearby attraction is the Edakkal Caves, known for their ancient petroglyphs that date back thousands of years. The picturesque Pookode Lake is also a short drive away, where visitors can enjoy boating and nature walks. For those interested in local culture, the heritage site of Thirunelli Temple, dedicated to Lord Vishnu, is within reach and holds historical significance. Each of these locations adds to the overall charm of the Wayanad region, making it a diverse destination for travelers.
