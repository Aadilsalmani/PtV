Eravikulam National Park

Eravikulam National Park

📍 Kerala(India)

Overview

Eravikulam National Park is located in the Western Ghats of Kerala, India, marked by the coordinates 10.14537006, 77.03659168. Established in 1978, the park covers an area of approximately 97 square kilometers and is recognized for its rich biodiversity and ecological significance. The park's elevation ranges from 1,200 to 2,695 meters above sea level, creating a variety of habitats that support numerous flora and fauna, including the Nilgiri Tahr, a species endemic to the region.

Why Visit

  • Home to the Nilgiri Tahr, a vulnerable species that is often seen grazing on the slopes.
  • Features rolling hills, lush grasslands, and a variety of endemic plant species, including the Neelakurinji, which blooms once every twelve years.
  • Offers panoramic views of the Western Ghats, making it a prime location for photography and nature observation.
  • Contains diverse wildlife, including various species of butterflies, birds, and small mammals, ideal for wildlife enthusiasts and bird watchers.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to Eravikulam National Park can engage in guided treks and nature walks that explore the park's unique ecosystems. The park features well-maintained trails that wind through the grasslands and forests, providing opportunities for close encounters with wildlife and stunning vistas of the surrounding mountains. The park's well-established visitor center offers informative exhibits about the local ecosystem and conservation efforts. The climate here is typically cool and pleasant, making it an inviting destination for outdoor activities.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to Eravikulam National Park, it is advisable to dress in layers, as temperatures can vary significantly throughout the day. Comfortable walking shoes are essential for navigating the trails. Visitors should also carry water and snacks, as facilities within the park are limited. Due to the park's focus on conservation, maintaining a safe distance from wildlife and adhering to park regulations is crucial for both visitor safety and the protection of the ecosystem.

Nearby Places

In close proximity to Eravikulam National Park, visitors can explore the town of Munnar, known for its tea plantations and breathtaking landscapes. The Attukal Waterfalls and the Mattupetty Dam are also within reach, offering additional opportunities for sightseeing and outdoor activities. Additionally, the region is dotted with various viewpoints that provide stunning views of the valleys and mountains characteristic of the Western Ghats.
