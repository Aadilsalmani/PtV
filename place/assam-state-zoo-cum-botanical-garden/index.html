Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden

Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden

📍 Assam(India)

Overview

The Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden, located at coordinates 26.16360689, 91.78137132, is a significant ecological and conservation area in Assam, India. Spanning over 432 hectares, this facility combines a zoological park with a botanical garden, making it a unique destination for nature enthusiasts and wildlife observers. Nestled in a lush green environment, the zoo is home to a diverse array of flora and fauna, reflecting the rich biodiversity of the region. Established in 1957, it aims to promote wildlife conservation and education while providing a habitat for various species endemic to Assam and beyond.

Why Visit

  • Home to over 900 animals, including endangered species such as the Indian one-horned rhinoceros and various primates.
  • Features a variety of plant species, with a focus on native flora from Assam and other regions, enhancing the botanical experience.
  • Offers well-maintained pathways for walking and observing wildlife in a naturalistic setting.
  • Includes educational exhibits and information boards that promote awareness about wildlife conservation and the importance of biodiversity.
  • Provides a peaceful retreat for visitors, making it suitable for picnics, family outings, and educational tours.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to the Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden can expect a well-organized layout that allows for easy exploration of both the zoo and botanical sections. The paths are designed to provide close encounters with various animal species while ensuring their welfare. Educational signage throughout the zoo enhances the experience by informing guests about the animals' habitats, diets, and conservation status. The botanical garden section offers a serene environment with diverse plant life, making it an ideal spot for relaxation and reflection amid nature. Various shaded areas and benches are available, allowing visitors to rest and enjoy the ambiance. The facility also hosts educational programs and workshops aimed at fostering a deeper understanding of wildlife and plant conservation.

Travel Tips

When planning a visit to this zoo and botanical garden, consider wearing comfortable walking shoes, as the area is expansive and involves substantial walking. Bringing water and snacks is advisable, especially for families, as there may be limited food options on-site. Early morning or late afternoon visits can provide a more pleasant experience due to cooler temperatures and increased animal activity. Additionally, visitors should check for any scheduled educational programs or guided tours that may enhance their experience. It is also recommended to carry a camera to capture the diverse wildlife and picturesque landscapes within the facility.

Nearby Places

Located in proximity to the Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden, visitors can explore several other attractions. The historic Kamakhya Temple, known for its religious significance, is a short distance away and attracts pilgrims and tourists alike. Additionally, the Umananda Island, situated on the Brahmaputra River, offers scenic beauty and tranquility. Nature lovers might also appreciate the nearby Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary, famous for its population of the Indian rhinoceros and rich birdlife. Each of these locations provides further opportunities for exploration and enjoyment of Assam's natural and cultural heritage.
