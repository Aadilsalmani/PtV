Nandankanan Zoological Park, Odisha(India)

Odisha(India)

Overview

Nandankanan Zoological Park, situated near the city of Bhubaneswar in Odisha, India, spans over 400 hectares and is renowned for its unique combination of a zoo, botanical garden, and a lake. Established in 1960, it was the first zoo in India to become a part of a sanctuary. The park is positioned adjacent to the Kanjia Lake, providing a serene backdrop for its inhabitants and visitors alike. The coordinates of this expansive park are 20.39576995 latitude and 85.82592626 longitude, placing it conveniently within reach for both local and international travelers.

Why Visit

  • Home to over 150 species of animals, including the white tiger, which is a significant attraction due to its rarity and conservation status.
  • The zoo operates a successful breeding program for endangered species, contributing to wildlife preservation efforts in India.
  • Featuring a diverse range of flora, the botanical garden showcases over 200 species of plants, making it a perfect place for botany enthusiasts.
  • Visitors can enjoy a safari ride that allows for up-close encounters with many species in a naturalistic setting.
  • Nandankanan is also known for its well-maintained infrastructure, including walking paths and viewing areas that enhance the visitor experience.

Visitor Experience

The experience at Nandankanan is designed to be educational and engaging. Upon arrival, visitors can explore the various enclosures that house both terrestrial and aquatic animals. The park is divided into sections based on habitats, allowing guests to learn about the ecosystems represented. Facilities such as the interpretation center provide insights into the conservation efforts and educational programs aimed at raising awareness about wildlife protection. The pleasant surroundings and the sound of nature add to the overall atmosphere, making it an enjoyable outing for families, students, and wildlife enthusiasts alike.

Travel Tips

To make the most of your visit to Nandankanan, plan your trip during the cooler months, as the weather can be quite warm in the summer. Arriving early in the day can help you avoid crowds and provide a better opportunity for animal sightings. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended due to the park's large area, and it's advisable to carry water and snacks, especially if you plan to spend several hours exploring. Additionally, consider visiting the zoo's educational exhibits and attending any scheduled talks or presentations to enhance your understanding of the wildlife and conservation efforts.

Nearby Places

In proximity to Nandankanan Zoological Park, visitors can explore several attractions. The Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves, ancient rock-cut caves dating back to the 2nd century BCE, offer a glimpse into the region's history. The Lingaraj Temple in Bhubaneswar, a remarkable example of Kalinga architecture, is another notable site nearby. Additionally, the Nandankanan Lake itself is suitable for boating and enjoying the natural scenery, while the surrounding areas boast several parks and gardens, providing a variety of leisure activities for visitors.
