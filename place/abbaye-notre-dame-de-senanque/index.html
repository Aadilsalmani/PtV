Abbaye Notre-Dame de Sénanque

Abbaye Notre-Dame de Sénanque

📍 France

Overview

Abbaye Notre-Dame de Sénanque is a stunning Cistercian monastery located in the picturesque valley of Sénanque, near the village of Gordes in Provence, France. Founded in 1148, it is one of the best-preserved examples of Cistercian architecture in the country. The abbey is renowned for its beautiful lavender fields, which bloom vibrantly during the summer months, providing a striking contrast to the stone structure and surrounding hills. The abbey is still home to a small community of monks who follow the Rule of Saint Benedict.

Why Visit

  • Experience a significant historical site that has been in continuous operation for over 850 years.
  • Admire the remarkable Romanesque architecture, characterized by its simplicity and harmony with the surrounding nature.
  • Explore the serene, cloistered gardens and the monks' accommodations, which reflect their commitment to a monastic life.
  • Witness the breathtaking lavender fields during the blooming season, typically from late June to early August.
  • Learn about the history and daily life of the monks through guided tours and informative displays.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to Abbaye Notre-Dame de Sénanque can take guided tours that delve into the abbey's rich history and architectural details. The tours often include access to the church, the cloister, and the monks' living quarters. The tranquil environment encourages reflection and contemplation, making it a perfect spot for those seeking a peaceful retreat. The abbey also hosts various cultural events and religious services, which visitors can attend to experience the spiritual life of the community. Photography opportunities abound, particularly in the lavender fields that surround the abbey, creating a picturesque setting that changes with the seasons.

Travel Tips

When visiting, it is advisable to wear comfortable footwear, as the terrain around the abbey can be uneven. The best time to visit for viewing the lavender fields is during the peak blooming season in July. Arriving early in the day can help avoid crowds and allow for a more peaceful experience. Photography is encouraged, but visitors should be mindful of the tranquility of the environment and the ongoing monastic life. Respect for the space is essential, especially during prayer times and services.

Nearby Places

The abbey is situated close to Gordes, a charming village known for its steep streets and stone houses that cling to the hillsides. Other nearby attractions include the Village des Bories, an open-air museum showcasing traditional dry-stone constructions, and the beautiful Luberon National Park, which offers numerous hiking trails and stunning landscapes. The region is also famous for its vineyards and olive groves, providing additional opportunities for exploration and local culinary experiences.
