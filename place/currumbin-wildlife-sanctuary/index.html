Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary

Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary

📍 Australia

Overview

Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Currumbin, Queensland, Australia, and spans over 27 hectares of lush rainforest and natural habitats. Established in 1947, this sanctuary is recognized for its commitment to wildlife conservation and education. It is home to a diverse array of native Australian animals, including kangaroos, koalas, and various species of birds. The sanctuary not only serves as a refuge for wildlife but also plays a crucial role in the rehabilitation of injured animals and the preservation of endangered species.

Why Visit

  • Home to over 1,000 native animals, including unique species like the Southern Cassowary and the Queensland Koala.
  • Features interactive animal encounters, allowing visitors to feed kangaroos and cuddle koalas under supervision.
  • Offers a variety of educational programs and wildlife presentations aimed at raising awareness about conservation efforts.
  • Set within a picturesque landscape, the sanctuary includes walking trails that meander through native flora, providing a serene environment for visitors.
  • Hosts the annual Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary's Animal Hospital open day, showcasing the sanctuary's commitment to animal care and rehabilitation.

Visitor Experience

Visitors to the sanctuary can engage in a range of experiences tailored to promote interaction with wildlife in a responsible manner. The sanctuary features daily presentations that highlight the behaviors and habitats of various species. One popular attraction is the free-flight bird show, where visitors can witness trained birds of prey and parrots in action. Guided tours are also available, offering insights into the conservation efforts and the importance of protecting Australia’s unique wildlife. The lush surroundings enhance the overall experience, making it an ideal spot for families, nature enthusiasts, and those interested in animal welfare.

Travel Tips

When visiting, it is advisable to wear comfortable walking shoes as the sanctuary's layout encourages exploration on foot. Sunscreen and hats are recommended due to the open areas that can get quite sunny. For those interested in animal encounters, check the schedule upon arrival to ensure participation in desired activities. Hydration is important, especially during warmer months, as visitors may spend several hours enjoying the various exhibits and presentations. Additionally, photography is encouraged, but it is essential to follow the sanctuary's guidelines regarding flash photography and animal interaction.

Nearby Places

Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary is situated near several attractions that complement a visit to the area. The picturesque Currumbin Beach is a short drive away, offering opportunities for swimming, surfing, and enjoying the coastal scenery. The Currumbin Rock Pools provide a tranquil setting for picnics and relaxation amidst nature. Additionally, the nearby Burleigh Heads National Park offers scenic walking trails and stunning coastal views, making it a perfect spot for hiking and exploring the diverse ecosystems of the Gold Coast region.
