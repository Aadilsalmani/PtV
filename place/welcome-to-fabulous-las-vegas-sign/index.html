Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas Sign

📍 USA

Overview

The Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas Sign is an iconic landmark located at the coordinates 36.08207376, -115.172782 in the United States. Positioned at the southern end of the Las Vegas Strip, this sign marks the entrance to one of the world’s most famous entertainment destinations. Erected in 1959, it is an emblem of the city’s vibrant nightlife and entertainment culture. The sign stands approximately 25 feet tall and features a distinctive Mid-Century Modern design, complete with neon lights and a starburst motif that captures the essence of Las Vegas.

Why Visit

  • Iconic Status: The sign is a symbol of Las Vegas and is recognized globally, making it a must-see for visitors.
  • Photographic Opportunity: Its unique design and bright colors provide an excellent backdrop for memorable photographs.
  • Historical Significance: The sign reflects the growth and transformation of Las Vegas from a small desert town to a major entertainment hub.
  • Accessibility: Located on a major thoroughfare, it is easily accessible for tourists exploring the Strip.
